# REGATTA — Cross-Domain Measurement and Fault-Injection Harness

**Canonical preprint:** [arXiv:2610.30924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30924), posted 22 October 2026.

**Title:** REGATTA: A Cross-Domain Measurement Framework and Fault-Injection Harness for Enterprise AI Agents

**Authors as listed on arXiv:** Luong Tuan Thanh, Abhijit Sanyal

**Venue status:** The arXiv record states accepted for publication at 2026 IEEE ICoDSE. Presentation and published proceedings are not independently established by this index.

**Research artifact:** [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23006005](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23006005), linked by the canonical preprint.

REGATTA distinguishes predicted fault exposure from execution observations and audits the source-cited instruments shared with the historical [RA-6](../RA-6/) corpus. The instruments require correction before legal use. Measurements describe the instruments as written; they do not certify legal compliance or authorize deployment.

This directory is a release guide. It links the canonical paper and archived artifact without mirroring the manuscript or changing the historical RA-6 files. Consult each external record for its license and release contents.
