## Research-question teaser

How do multi-agent systems coordinate under realistic enterprise constraints — bounded context windows, divergent ontology grounding, latency budgets, and partial observability — and which coordination patterns degrade gracefully when the underlying LLMs are heterogeneous in capability? RA-1 instruments the FAOS A2A coordination layer across a battery of enterprise workflows (regulated banking, manufacturing dispatch, healthcare triage) to characterize empirical breakdown points and recovery patterns.
